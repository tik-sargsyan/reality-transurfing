Part One

The Twenty Core Concepts

The model in twenty frames, in the order the book builds it. Each summary is original commentary.

01The Alternatives Space

An infinite static field holding every scenario that could ever occur. Nothing is created in it, only illuminated. Past and future sit fixed, and the sensation of time comes from which frame is currently lit.

HookOne lit room in an endless dark archive of unlit rooms. The room was always there. You did not build it, you switched it on.

02Life Lines

Sectors of similar quality chained together, because cause sits close to effect. Your life runs stably along one line until something changes the script enough to move you to another.

HookRails of light through the field, each stitching cells of one colour. A switch-junction lets you cross to a warmer rail.

RuleYou are always on the line matching your thought energy. Change the outlook and you move.

03Material Realisation

Thought energy of a given character illuminates the matching sector and makes it physical. Every living thing does this continuously, so shared reality is the sum of everyone's transmission.

HookA projector beam from your head making one cell solid while all the rest stay ghosts.

04The Pendulum

When a group thinks alike, their energy accumulates into a self-sustaining structure that then develops independently and imposes its own laws. It feeds on emotion, any strong reaction being a payment, and it grows or dies with the number of its adherents.

HookA cathedral bell swinging over a crowd, fed by threads running from their chests, lifting them off their feet.

RuleIgnore it. Not avoid, not resist. Both of those are still feeding it. The real damage is not the energy taken, it is being steered onto a life you never chose.

05Excess Potential

A local tension in the field, created whenever you assign disproportionate meaning to something. Desire is the archetype: wanting intensely tries to pull a thing to where it is not, and that creates a pressure differential.

HookA perfectly flat sea with one blister of pressure straining upward, made by one person wanting one thing too much.

06Balanced Forces

Whatever creates the disturbance summons the forces that eliminate it, and because you created it, those forces act on you. Since the tension can be resolved in either direction, the result is often the exact reverse of the intention.

HookWind converging from every side to crush the blister flat, and it is the person who gets blown over, not the world.

RuleThis is the mechanism behind the feeling that some perverse law makes things go wrong for no visible reason.

07Importance

The energy that creates every excess potential. Inner importance is overrating yourself in either direction, since inflation and self-deprecation generate identical tension at opposite polarity. Outer importance is overrating an object or an event.

HookThe same plank, on the ground and between two rooftops. Same width, same walk. Only the meaning changed.

RuleThe more important the goal, the less likely the achievement. Obstacles are built from importance, so deflate them rather than overcome them.

08Polarisation

Excess potential on its own is inert. It turns destructive the moment the inflated valuation is set against something else, by comparison or by condition. Dependent relationships are the interpersonal form, recognisable by the conditional: if you do this, then I will do that.

HookTwo figures joined by a taut cord of conditions, the ground cracking between them, currents already moving in to slam them together or tear them apart.

RuleUnconditional love is admiration without worship and love without the claim to possess.

09The Induced Transition

Crises develop as spirals: seed, escalation, emotional peak, discharge, calm. A structure captures a crowd's attention and swings harder, and the instant an individual reacts they enter the field of the spiral and get pulled onto lines where they play victim.

HookA whirlpool made of headlines and shouting faces. Everyone who turned their head is being dragged down. One person walks past without looking.

RuleYou are not pulled in by the event. You are pulled in by your reaction to news of it.

10The World Layer

Each person materialises a private layer of reality, and the layers stack into the shared world. Your layer takes on a character from your attitudes, which is why the model describes it in weather terms: bright and clear, or overcast, or storming.

HookStacked glass panes, each with someone painting their own weather onto their own sheet. Yours is the one in front.

11The Alternatives Flow

Chains of cause and effect form a current, and the current takes the path of least resistance, so it already contains the efficient solution to most problems. Fighting it burns enormous energy and manufactures new problems.

HookOne figure thrashing upstream and getting nowhere, one floating and covering ten times the distance for none of the effort.

RuleShift the centre of gravity from control to observation. Most small problems resolve themselves if you stop interfering.

12Guiding Signs

Markers that appear when the flow is about to bend onto a qualitatively different line. A sign is not identified by what it is but by the flicker of something being subtly off. If it does not snag your attention, nothing has changed.

HookA crow, a fallen lantern, a free-standing door, all glowing faintly, all exactly at the bend in the river.

RuleThe most reliable sign is your own state, and it is asymmetric. Discomfort is an unambiguous no. Comfort is not a yes. If you have to talk yourself into it, the answer has already been given.

13Inner and Outer Intention

Intention is the resolve to have and to act, and it splits in two. Inner intention is the resolve to act, attention on your own movement, working by direct effort. Outer intention is the resolve to have, attention on the goal realising itself, working by selection rather than force.

HookShoving a mountain and failing, versus standing still with open hands while the mountain glides toward you.

RuleInner intention achieves a goal. Outer intention selects it. Stop pushing the world and let it deliver.

14Unity of Heart and Mind

The mind has will but cannot feel outer intention. The heart can feel outer intention but has no will. Neither works alone, so the entire game is getting the two to agree.

HookTwo glowing centres in one silhouette locking into resonance, and the interference tearing open a window onto somewhere sunlit.

RuleThis is why dread is so effective. Fear is one of the few states in which heart and mind agree completely, which is exactly why worst expectations arrive so reliably.

15The Slide

A distorted picture carried in your mind and unknowingly projected onto everything you see, colouring your perception and over time your circumstances. Every slide rests on importance, and when the importance goes the slide stops existing.

HookA projector inside a glass head warping the street it lights, and a hand quietly swapping the transparency for a better one.

RuleDo not fight a slide, replace it. And visualise the process of growth, not the finished result. The result image gives direction; the process is what produces movement.

16The Wave of Fortune

Favorable life lines cluster in the same way unfavorable ones do. Land on the edge of a cluster and, if you let the first stroke of luck actually register, you slide deeper into it by inertia and the luck compounds.

HookOne golden swell rolling through a slate ocean, with dark tendrils reaching up to pull the surfer off the crest.

RuleIf a bad patch follows the first good thing, something has hooked you and pulled you off. The hard part is not understanding this, it is remembering it in the moment.

17Coordination of Intention

Every event forks into a favorable and an unfavorable branch, and your relationship to the event decides which branch you take. Since chronic negativists demonstrably receive their worst expectations, the same machinery run deliberately in reverse should work.

HookA rail splitting into grey storm and amber light, with a traveller's hand on the lever, calmly pleased about bad news.

RuleMisfortune does not chain to the first misfortune. It chains to your reaction to it. Adopt the deliberately foolish habit of taking joy in setbacks.

18Frailing

Abandon the intention to get, replace it with the intention to give, and you receive the thing you released. The mechanism is not mystical: everyone's drive is organised around their own sense of worth, so raising theirs sets their intention working on your behalf at no cost to them.

HookA giver whose own chest fills brighter than the light they handed over, while the other person turns and offers back the thing that was wanted all along.

RuleIn conversation people are not assessing how interesting you are. They are assessing how well you might serve their sense of worth. Show interest rather than trying to be interesting.

19Goals and Doors

A foreign goal arrives dressed as prestige, attracts partly by seeming unattainable, makes you prove something, serves someone else's interest, and feels like an obligation. Your own goal turns life into a celebration and fulfils your other wishes as side effects along the way. Your door is the specific path to it.

HookA corridor of prestigious locked doors with queues fighting to enter, and one plain unqueued door opening to a key shaped like your own silhouette.

RuleAchievement is not the hard part, identification is. A goal that weighs on you is probably not yours, however attractive it looks. Do not think about the means until the goal is clear.

20The Dual Mirror

The world reflects your relationship to it. The reflection forms in the unity of heart and mind, it arrives with a delay, and it registers the content of your relationship while ignoring its sign.

HookA reflection lagging a beat behind the person, who has stopped rearranging the glass and is stepping around its edge to stand where the maker stands.

RuleThe mirror does not hear that you do not want something, it only hears the something. And the delay is structural, so absent early results are expected. Impatience at that point is what breaks the practice.

Part Two

The Seven Mirror Rules

Chapter XVIII sets out seven principles for working with the dual mirror. Each is quoted here in Zeland's own words, then illustrated.

IThe World Reflects

The book's words“the world is like a mirror that reflects your relationship to it”

HookThe glass holds no body, only a landscape whose weather matches the posture in front of it. Where the figure's shadow touches the surface, the valley beyond goes grey.

RuleThe world answers your relationship to it, not your effort. Change what you hold toward it and the reflection has nothing else to copy.

IIHeart and Mind Agree

The book's words“reflection is formed in the unity of heart and mind”

HookTwo beams leave one body, a cool lattice from the head and a warm glow from the chest. The mirror stays fogged everywhere except the narrow band where the two lock into phase.

RuleA wish the mind argues for and the heart refuses never reaches the glass. Only what both hold without conflict forms an image.

IIIThe Delay

The book's words“there is a delay in the reaction of the dual mirror”

HookYou already stand tall. The reflection is still hunched, dragging itself forward through a trail of ghost positions like slow honey.

RuleThe lag is structural, not a verdict. Early results are supposed to be absent, which is exactly why impatience at that point is what breaks the practice. If unity is out of reach, take the fortress by siege.

IVContent Not Direction

The book's words“The mirror cites the content of the relationship but ignores its orientation”

HookThe gesture of refusal dissolves into vapour at the surface of the glass while the thing being refused passes straight through and multiplies on the far side.

RuleThe mirror registers the subject of your attention and discards the sign on it. Fighting a thing and wanting it feed the same reflection.

VMove the Image

The book's words“switch your attention from the reflection to the image”

HookOn one side of the glass, hands sliding uselessly across the surface trying to rearrange what is in it. On the other, a person with their back turned holding a small amber slide, and the whole mirror filled with it.

RuleA reflection cannot be moved by touching it. Think about what you are moving toward rather than what you are trying to get away from, and the glass follows.

VIRelease Your Grip

The book's words“release your grip and allow the world to move with the alternatives flow”

HookWhite knuckles on the frame with the current piling up against them, and the same hands opening, the same river running clean and fast.

RuleThe paradox is exact: abandoning the position of control is what gives you control. Keep the goal, drop the script for how it has to arrive.

VIIEvery Reflection Positive

The book's words“embrace any reflection as positive”

HookA cracked, storm-coloured reflection, with gold spreading through the fractures outward from the single point where a calm gaze meets the glass.

RuleFrom inside the mirror you can never know whether a turn is for your good or against it, so the reading is yours to make. Declaring it good is what makes it so.

Part Three

The Ten Techniques

Chapter XIX lists ten techniques for shaping reality. The target slide is the central one; the other nine strengthen it. Zeland's own dosage: at least half an hour a day if you genuinely intend to reach the goal, held in the background the rest of the time, over months rather than days, and never forced. Use the ones you actually enjoy and drop the rest.

IThe Target Slide

The book's words“The target slide is the central instrument of Transurfing, visualising a picture in which the goal has already been achieved”

PracticeHold the end state, never the path. You are inside it: what you are doing, what you feel, what is around you. No script for how it arrived and no conditions on the means. Run it at least half an hour a day if you genuinely intend to reach the goal, and keep it playing quietly in the background the rest of the time.

HookA cinema screen abandoned in the dark with an empty chair, and footprints leading from it into the room the picture became.

IIVisualising the Process

The book's words“everything is going extremely well and today I am doing everything better than yesterday and tomorrow will be better than today”

PracticeFor once you know the path and are already walking it. Picture the current link growing rather than the finished result. The target slide sets the direction; this adds speed. Zeland's image for it is working the oar in harmony with the flow.

HookAn apple tree with a ladder of amber rungs up the trunk, and a gardener kneeling to water only today's rung.

IIIEnergy Channels

The book's words“Focusing your attention partly on the currents of energy launch the slide and play it to your heart's content”

PracticeImagine two arrows leaving the solar plexus about a metre to front and back, then turn them like a key so the front points up and the rear points down. Two currents run the spine, one out into the sky, one down into the ground. Hold them loosely, then run the slide. Best done walking somewhere quiet.

HookA walking figure with the key just turned, one column of light rising from the crown and one sinking into the earth.

IVThe Frame

The book's words“From time to time turn it on in your memory for an instant as you would turn on a light”

PracticeTake one characteristic fragment of the slide, its indispensable attribute, and turn it over a few times until it compresses into a single flash image plus the feeling that comes with it. Give it a short name. Then switch it on for an instant whenever it naturally occurs to you, with no effort at all.

HookOne small burning instant suspended in a vast dark, hands closed on a ship's helm, a single thread running from it to the horizon.

VThe Explosion Wave

The book's words“imagining that a sphere is expanding outwards from your body in all directions as if your energy field had suddenly exploded”

PracticeShape the frame, then immediately let a sphere burst outward from you in every direction, as far as you can imagine it. Repeat until you are tired of it. The thought is not lost; it simply answers late.

HookA calm figure at the centre of a shockwave of amber light, the scene of a life printed faintly on its surface as it travels.

VIOuter Sphere

The book's words“The sphere will serve as a kind of antennae for the transmission of thought energy and this will substantially strengthen the efficiency of the slide”

PracticeDo not push a sphere out from yourself. Imagine one that is not yours, five to seven metres out, and let it draw you. When you feel the springy resistance, keep part of your attention on it and run the slide through it. This is the drill that converts inner intention into outer.

HookA figure drawn outward by a membrane that is not theirs, every filament pointing inward from the sphere to the body.

VIIScenery Reduction

The book's words“Whatever you find yourself thinking about, whatever you are doing, return your attention to the goal. Make a background image of your slide”

PracticeReduce every stray thought to the common denominator of the goal, finishing each chain with a fragment of the slide. Then look at what is physically in front of you through that slide until the real scene takes on the colour of the one you want.

HookAn ordinary park at dusk wearing a sunlit garden like a second skin, every drifting thought-thread bending into the same picture.

VIIITarget Amalgam

The book's words“although the ‘how’ may be unbeknownst to me, everything will work its way through”

PracticeFor when visualisation has turned into a chore. Forcing it generates excess potential and balanced forces cancel the work. Hand the job to your world, ask it to carry you, then enjoy the slide with no obligation to work on it. Remind the world now and then what you expect, and still do your part in the physical world.

HookA person carried by the ground itself, toolbag set down at the start of the path, hands open and empty.

IXFocusing Intention

The book's words“The magical power of any given object lies in one's relationship to it”

PracticeTake a concrete step that proves the intention is serious, the way setting an alarm makes you wake a few minutes before it rings. Read the catalogues, walk into the shops, choose the furniture. Hang the intention on a hook, an object or a ritual or an act, so it stops being vapour.

HookA hand hanging a cloud of amber light onto a small brass key beside a set alarm clock, the diffuse vapour condensing into a point that casts shadows.

XIntegration

The book's words“everything was wonderful and would get even better; that everything was simply unfolding as it should”

PracticeThe endpoint of the whole set, taken from a reader's letter Zeland quotes. Hold that one integral feeling and he says outright you can forget the techniques, because this is the state they exist to produce.

HookA figure walking at dawn with no apparatus anywhere in the frame, one even light belonging equally to sky, ground, water and person.

Part Four

Every Chapter Summary

The end-of-chapter summaries from all nineteen chapters, quoted verbatim from Reality Transurfing, Steps I–V. The afterword carries no summary. Open any chapter to read it.

I.The Alternatives Model
  • Reality is manifest in an infinite variety of forms.
  • Variability is the world’s most fundamental quality.
  • Any model of the universe represents just one manifestation of reality.
  • Any branch of knowledge bases itself on one chosen aspect of the manifestation of reality.
  • Your choice is always realized. You always receive what you choose.
  • The alternatives space is an information field recording all that was, is and ever will be.
  • The information field contains potential alternative scenarios to any event.
  • Each alternative has a script and scenery.
  • Space can be divided into sectors and each sector contains an alternative scenario of an event.
  • The greater the distance between sectors, the more the alternatives will differ.
  • Sectors with roughly uniform parameters are arranged in a life line.
  • Material realisation moves through space like a bundle of density.
  • The radiation of thought energy materializes an alternative.
  • Every organism contributes to the creation of material realization.
  • When the parameters of thought energy change, a shift occurs to a different life line.
  • You cannot change the fact that scenery exists, but you can choose a different type of scenery.
  • You do not have to fight for happiness. You simply have to choose an alternative reality that is more to your liking.
II.Pendulums
  • A pendulum is created by the energy of a group of people thinking in the same way.
  • A pendulum is an energy based information structure.
  • A pendulum maintains the thought energy of the adherent at its own vibrational frequency.
  • A harsh battle for adherents takes place between the pendulums.
  • A destructive pendulum forces goals onto its adherents that are not of their own making.
  • A pendulum plays on human emotion, thereby catching the individual in their web.
  • If there is something that you really do not want it will appear in your life.
  • To free oneself from a pendulum means to give it no place in your life.
  • To give something no place in your life does not mean to try and avoid it; it means to ignore it.
  • To bring a pendulum to rest you have to change the established game script.
  • Positive visualization will gently bring the swing of a human pendulum to rest.
  • The energy of a stilled pendulum becomes available to you.
  • Problems are solved by defeating or stopping the pendulum that created them.
  • To solve a problem, rent yourself out.
  • To avoid getting stuck in a suspended state, find your own pendulums.
  • It is essential to acquire the habit of remembering what you know.
III.The Wave of Fortune
  • The wave of good fortune is an accumulation of favourable life lines in the alternatives space.
  • A cascade of good luck follows only if you have let yourself become imbued with the joyful energy evoked by the first stroke of luck.
  • Destructive pendulums will try to coax you away from the wave of fortune.
  • When you overcome dependence on pendulums you win your freedom.
  • By taking on and transmitting negative energy you create your own hell.
  • By accepting and transmitting positive energy you create your own heaven.
  • Your thoughts always return to you like a boomerang.
  • Pendulums cannot throw you off the wave of fortune once remembering has become a habit.
  • The habit of remembering is developed through consistent practice.
IV.Balance
  • Excess potential is only created when you attribute importance to your assessment.
  • Only the importance you personally attribute to something imbues your assessment with energy.
  • The force of the excess potential created, increases if your assessment represents a distortion of reality.
  • The impact of balanced forces is aimed at eliminating excess potential.
  • The action of balanced forces is often the opposite of the original intention that created the potential.
  • When renting yourself out, instruct your inner Guardian be help you remain impeccable.
  • Discontent and judgement will always turn balanced forces against you.
  • It is essential that habits of negative response be substituted with a transmission of positive energy.
  • Unconditional love is admiration without worship or the need to possess.
  • Setting conditions and making comparisons leads to dependent relationships.
  • Dependent relationships create excess potential.
  • Idealization and overestimation always end in disillusionment.
  • To experience reciprocal love you must let go of the right to possess.
  • You always have to pay for expressions of contempt and vanity.
  • Let go of the need to assert your superiority.
  • Striving to hide shortcomings creates the opposite effect.
  • Your positive qualities compensate for any inadequacies.
  • The greater the importance of your goal, the less likely you are to achieve it.
  • Desires are realized when they are free of excess potential created by projected importance and dependency.
  • Let go of feelings of guilt and the need to justify your actions.
  • To let go of guilt, it is enough to give yourself permission to be yourself.
  • No-one has the right to judge you. You have the right to be yourself.
  • Money comes of its own accord as an attribute on the path to your goal.
  • Greet money with love and attentiveness and part with it without regret.
  • Having let go of inner and outer importance you acquire freedom of choice.
  • The projection of importance is the only obstacle on the way to fulfilling your desires.
  • Do not try to overcome obstacles; reduce the amount of importance you project onto them.
  • Take care without worrying.
V.The Induced Transition
  • Everyone creates the separate layer of their own world.
  • The world of humanity consists of individual layers, placed one on top of the other.
  • When a person emanates negative energy they compromise the quality of their own life layer.
  • Aggression is mistakenly taken to be a strength and dissatisfaction as a normal reaction.
  • Reacting to negative events induces a shift to negative life lines.
  • An induced transition is accompanied by a negative event in a personal life layer.
  • Do not allow negative information into your layer.
  • “Do not allow” does not mean strive to avoid, but rather, to intentionally ignore, to express no interest.
VI.The Alternatives Flow
  • The mind interprets information using a set of established designations.
  • The heart does not think or speak. It feels and knows.
  • The mind is only capable of building a relatively new version of a house from old bricks.
  • Fundamentally new discoveries originate in unrealised sectors of the alternatives space.
  • The heart serves as a medium between new information and the mind.
  • The heart perceives unrealised information as knowledge beyond interpretation.
  • A discovery is made when the mind is able to interpret information accessed by the heart.
  • The mind is capable of perfectly assessing one’s level of inner comfort.
  • Learn to pay attention to your inner state.
  • By foregoing the projection of importance you are free to choose your destiny.
  • Freedom of choice means being free not to ask, demand or fight for what you want but simply to go out and get it.
  • The information structure is organised in chains of cause and effect.
  • Chains of cause and effect generate the alternatives flow.
  • Paths of least resistance are organised into separate streams.
  • Streams in the alternatives flow contain the solutions to all our problems.
  • Inner and outer importance jolts the mind out of the optimal stream.
  • In the alternatives flow it is not the streams but your mind that brings you to the edge of the waterfall.
  • Everything works a lot more simply than you think. Yield to this simplicity.
  • What works is not the omen itself but your attitude to the omen.
  • Guiding signs indicate a possible bend in the flow of alternatives.
  • Life lines differ from each other in quality.
  • Signs put us on our guard because they appear during a shift to a different life line.
  • Signs are characterised by their tendency to evoke the feeling that something is not right.
  • Spontaneous phrases can be perceived as instructions.
  • The level of inner peace one feels represents a clear sign.
  • If you have to talk yourself into something it means that the heart is saying ‘no’.
  • If it is possible to change a decision that causes you to feel uncomfortable in your gut, do so.
  • Soften your grip on things and allow unforeseen events into the script.
  • Projected importance hinders the mind from accepting a deviation from the script.
  • The mind strives to control the flow itself rather than its own manner of moving with the flow.
  • Shift the centre of gravity from control to observation.
  • Having let go of attachment to control you will find you have genuine control of a situation.
  • If you move harmoniously with the flow of alternatives the world will meet you half way.
VII.Intention
  • In conscious dreaming the mind controls the game script.
  • Dreaming is the virtual journey of the soul through the alternatives space.
  • Dreams should not be interpreted as signs.
  • The soul will not necessarily return if it flies into a realized sector of the alternatives space.
  • It is not desire that is realized but intention, i.e. the resoluteness to have and to act.
  • Desire is the concentration of attention on the goal.
  • Inner intention is the concentration of attention on the process of moving in the direction of the goal.
  • Outer intention is the concentration of attention on how the goal realizes itself.
  • The goal is achieved by inner intention and by outer intention simply chosen.
  • Inner intention strives to impact the outside world directly.
  • Outer intention gives the green light for the autonomous realization of the goal.
  • The laws of natural science only work in one given sector of the alternatives space.
  • Outer intention works by moving through different sectors of the space.
  • Outer intention is the unity of heart and mind.
  • Imagination takes part in dreaming only to the extent that generates the initial idea.
  • The heart and mind are united in negative expectation, which is why they are easily realized.
  • In reality a dream continues in waking to some degree or another.
  • If you want to exercise control over outer intention you have to wake up.
  • Whilst you live without conscious awareness, reality cannot be controlled, it just ‘happens’.
  • Any game must be played with an element of detachment like a participating observer.
  • Awareness is achieved by being detached from the game.
  • Detachment presupposes vigilance and complete mental clarity.
  • Conscious awareness is not so much about control as observation.
  • Control should be exercised only to the extent that it allows desired scripts into your life.
  • In order to choose the necessary script you have to imagine that it will be exactly as you desire it.
  • Inner intention is resoluteness in the decision to act.
  • Outer intention is resoluteness in the decision to have.
  • Outer intention is the power that makes Transurfing possible.
  • In order to reduce the importance associated with the goal the possibility of failure must be accepted at the outset.
  • Once you have accepted the possibility of failure, do not think, just move in the direction of your goal.
VIII.Slides
  • Illusions are not the result of fantasy but the perception of a different reality.
  • People present in the material world are capable of perceiving other realities.
  • Inner convictions can distort a person’s objective perception of the world.
  • A slide is something you carry around in your own mind that others are not.
  • Slides distort the perception of true reality.
  • People have a tendency to hang their projections on others around them.
  • Importance lies at the foundation of all slides.
  • As soon as importance disappears the slide ceases to exist.
  • Outer intention gradually but consistently realizes the content of the slide.
  • Stop battling with yourself and switch your attention from the negative to the positive.
  • Create a positive slide that is pleasing both to your heart and mind.
  • Look at your slide more often each time embellishing it with more detail.
  • Never portray an image in your slide that is copied from another person.
  • You will never receive anything that does not trigger your will to have .
  • Allow yourself the luxury of deserving the absolute best.
  • The will to have is the immutable knowledge that you deserve what you want and that it is your choice.
  • Positive slides help you to push open the limits of your comfort zone to include things that previously seemed implausible.
  • Do not treat the slide as if it were just a picture; live it, at least in the virtual world.
  • Allow in any information relating to the world of your dream.
  • It is not contemplation of the result but visualisation of the process that moves you towards your goal.
  • Rather than contemplating the result imagine the process of birth and growth.
  • In Transurfing, visualisation is understood to be the act of focusing on a mental image of the realization process of a current link in the transfer chain.
  • If it is not clear to you how to achieve your goal, focus on visualising the slide instead.
  • The slide will take you in the necessary direction.
IX.The Heart and Mind
  • The mind has will but is incapable of feeling outer intention.
  • The heart can feel outer intention but is not capable of will.
  • When heart and mind are united they harness the will of outer intention.
  • Your heart is just as good as the heart of others. You deserve the absolute best.
  • You have everything you need. All you have to do is use it.
  • Stars are born independently but pendulums light them up.
  • Pendulums hide the fact that every individual has unique abilities.
  • The rule ‘Do as I do”, creates the pendulums’ widely accepted stereotypes.
  • Every soul has its own individual ‘star’ sector.
  • If the mind allows it to, the heart will find its sector.
  • Allow yourself the boldness to sneeze at stereotypes.
  • Allow yourself the boldness to believe in the unlimited capabilities of your soul.
  • Allow yourself the boldness to exercise the right to your own amazing individuality.
  • Keep the joy and give the pride to your Angel.
  • Human thought and behaviour is conditioned by dependency on pendulums.
  • Keep importance to a minimum and act with conscious awareness.
  • Do not attribute anything excessively meaning.
  • You do not need importance; pendulums do.
  • It is not effort and steadfastness but conscious intention that keeps importance at zero.
  • Fraile characterises the individual essence of the human soul.
  • In chasing after other peoples’ standards the mind becomes all the more divorced from the heart.
  • You will acquire many hidden virtues by attuning your mind to the soul’s Fraile.
  • In a state of unity the heart sings and the mind rubs its hands in satisfaction.
