Reality Transurfing

Twenty Core Concepts

A visual memory aid for the model set out in Reality Transurfing, Steps I–V by Vadim Zeland. The summaries below are original commentary, not extracts. For the argument itself, the case studies and the author's voice, read the book.

01The Alternatives Space

An infinite static field holding every scenario that could ever occur. Nothing is created in it, only illuminated. Past and future sit fixed, and the sensation of time comes from which frame is currently lit.

HookOne lit room in an endless dark archive of unlit rooms. The room was always there. You did not build it, you switched it on.

02Life Lines

Sectors of similar quality chained together, because cause sits close to effect. Your life runs stably along one line until something changes the script enough to move you to another.

HookRails of light through the field, each stitching cells of one colour. A switch-junction lets you cross to a warmer rail.

RuleYou are always on the line matching your thought energy. Change the outlook and you move.

03Material Realisation

Thought energy of a given character illuminates the matching sector and makes it physical. Every living thing does this continuously, so shared reality is the sum of everyone's transmission.

HookA projector beam from your head making one cell solid while all the rest stay ghosts.

04The Pendulum

When a group thinks alike, their energy accumulates into a self-sustaining structure that then develops independently and imposes its own laws. It feeds on emotion, any strong reaction being a payment, and it grows or dies with the number of its adherents.

HookA cathedral bell swinging over a crowd, fed by threads running from their chests, lifting them off their feet.

RuleIgnore it. Not avoid, not resist. Both of those are still feeding it. The real damage is not the energy taken, it is being steered onto a life you never chose.

05Excess Potential

A local tension in the field, created whenever you assign disproportionate meaning to something. Desire is the archetype: wanting intensely tries to pull a thing to where it is not, and that creates a pressure differential.

HookA perfectly flat sea with one blister of pressure straining upward, made by one person wanting one thing too much.

06Balanced Forces

Whatever creates the disturbance summons the forces that eliminate it, and because you created it, those forces act on you. Since the tension can be resolved in either direction, the result is often the exact reverse of the intention.

HookWind converging from every side to crush the blister flat, and it is the person who gets blown over, not the world.

RuleThis is the mechanism behind the feeling that some perverse law makes things go wrong for no visible reason.

07Importance

The energy that creates every excess potential. Inner importance is overrating yourself in either direction, since inflation and self-deprecation generate identical tension at opposite polarity. Outer importance is overrating an object or an event.

HookThe same plank, on the ground and between two rooftops. Same width, same walk. Only the meaning changed.

RuleThe more important the goal, the less likely the achievement. Obstacles are built from importance, so deflate them rather than overcome them.

08Polarisation

Excess potential on its own is inert. It turns destructive the moment the inflated valuation is set against something else, by comparison or by condition. Dependent relationships are the interpersonal form, recognisable by the conditional: if you do this, then I will do that.

HookTwo figures joined by a taut cord of conditions, the ground cracking between them, currents already moving in to slam them together or tear them apart.

RuleUnconditional love is admiration without worship and love without the claim to possess.

09The Induced Transition

Crises develop as spirals: seed, escalation, emotional peak, discharge, calm. A structure captures a crowd's attention and swings harder, and the instant an individual reacts they enter the field of the spiral and get pulled onto lines where they play victim.

HookA whirlpool made of headlines and shouting faces. Everyone who turned their head is being dragged down. One person walks past without looking.

RuleYou are not pulled in by the event. You are pulled in by your reaction to news of it.

10The World Layer

Each person materialises a private layer of reality, and the layers stack into the shared world. Your layer takes on a character from your attitudes, which is why the model describes it in weather terms: bright and clear, or overcast, or storming.

HookStacked glass panes, each with someone painting their own weather onto their own sheet. Yours is the one in front.

11The Alternatives Flow

Chains of cause and effect form a current, and the current takes the path of least resistance, so it already contains the efficient solution to most problems. Fighting it burns enormous energy and manufactures new problems.

HookOne figure thrashing upstream and getting nowhere, one floating and covering ten times the distance for none of the effort.

RuleShift the centre of gravity from control to observation. Most small problems resolve themselves if you stop interfering.

12Guiding Signs

Markers that appear when the flow is about to bend onto a qualitatively different line. A sign is not identified by what it is but by the flicker of something being subtly off. If it does not snag your attention, nothing has changed.

HookA crow, a fallen lantern, a free-standing door, all glowing faintly, all exactly at the bend in the river.

RuleThe most reliable sign is your own state, and it is asymmetric. Discomfort is an unambiguous no. Comfort is not a yes. If you have to talk yourself into it, the answer has already been given.

13Inner and Outer Intention

Intention is the resolve to have and to act, and it splits in two. Inner intention is the resolve to act, attention on your own movement, working by direct effort. Outer intention is the resolve to have, attention on the goal realising itself, working by selection rather than force.

HookShoving a mountain and failing, versus standing still with open hands while the mountain glides toward you.

RuleInner intention achieves a goal. Outer intention selects it. Stop pushing the world and let it deliver.

14Unity of Heart and Mind

The mind has will but cannot feel outer intention. The heart can feel outer intention but has no will. Neither works alone, so the entire game is getting the two to agree.

HookTwo glowing centres in one silhouette locking into resonance, and the interference tearing open a window onto somewhere sunlit.

RuleThis is why dread is so effective. Fear is one of the few states in which heart and mind agree completely, which is exactly why worst expectations arrive so reliably.

15The Slide

A distorted picture carried in your mind and unknowingly projected onto everything you see, colouring your perception and over time your circumstances. Every slide rests on importance, and when the importance goes the slide stops existing.

HookA projector inside a glass head warping the street it lights, and a hand quietly swapping the transparency for a better one.

RuleDo not fight a slide, replace it. And visualise the process of growth, not the finished result. The result image gives direction; the process is what produces movement.

16The Wave of Fortune

Favorable life lines cluster in the same way unfavorable ones do. Land on the edge of a cluster and, if you let the first stroke of luck actually register, you slide deeper into it by inertia and the luck compounds.

HookOne golden swell rolling through a slate ocean, with dark tendrils reaching up to pull the surfer off the crest.

RuleIf a bad patch follows the first good thing, something has hooked you and pulled you off. The hard part is not understanding this, it is remembering it in the moment.

17Coordination of Intention

Every event forks into a favorable and an unfavorable branch, and your relationship to the event decides which branch you take. Since chronic negativists demonstrably receive their worst expectations, the same machinery run deliberately in reverse should work.

HookA rail splitting into grey storm and amber light, with a traveller's hand on the lever, calmly pleased about bad news.

RuleMisfortune does not chain to the first misfortune. It chains to your reaction to it. Adopt the deliberately foolish habit of taking joy in setbacks.

18Frailing

Abandon the intention to get, replace it with the intention to give, and you receive the thing you released. The mechanism is not mystical: everyone's drive is organised around their own sense of worth, so raising theirs sets their intention working on your behalf at no cost to them.

HookA giver whose own chest fills brighter than the light they handed over, while the other person turns and offers back the thing that was wanted all along.

RuleIn conversation people are not assessing how interesting you are. They are assessing how well you might serve their sense of worth. Show interest rather than trying to be interesting.

19Goals and Doors

A foreign goal arrives dressed as prestige, attracts partly by seeming unattainable, makes you prove something, serves someone else's interest, and feels like an obligation. Your own goal turns life into a celebration and fulfils your other wishes as side effects along the way. Your door is the specific path to it.

HookA corridor of prestigious locked doors with queues fighting to enter, and one plain unqueued door opening to a key shaped like your own silhouette.

RuleAchievement is not the hard part, identification is. A goal that weighs on you is probably not yours, however attractive it looks. Do not think about the means until the goal is clear.

20The Dual Mirror

The world reflects your relationship to it. The reflection forms in the unity of heart and mind, it arrives with a delay, and it registers the content of your relationship while ignoring its sign.

HookA reflection lagging a beat behind the person, who has stopped rearranging the glass and is stepping around its edge to stand where the maker stands.

RuleThe mirror does not hear that you do not want something, it only hears the something. And the delay is structural, so absent early results are expected. Impatience at that point is what breaks the practice.

Concepts from Reality Transurfing, Steps I–V by Vadim Zeland. Summaries and illustrations by Tigran Sargsyan. Images generated with Nano Banana Pro.

Zeland's system is metaphysical rather than scientific and makes no falsifiable claims. Read it as a set of psychological heuristics wrapped in a cosmology.
